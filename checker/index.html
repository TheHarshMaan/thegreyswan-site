Manuscript Pre-Submission Checker

Manuscript checker · Reference checker

Check a manuscript the way a journal screening desk does before peer review: structure, abbreviations, figure and table citations, ethics and consent statements, declarations, references and citation style (APA, AMA, Vancouver, Harvard, IEEE) and, for double-blind journals, anonymisation. Choose your journal's profile, or adjust the rules to match its guidelines.

Private by design. The checker runs inside your browser. Your manuscripts are never uploaded, stored or shared: they don't leave your computer.
A screening aid, not a verdict. It flags issues for a person to review. It does not judge the science, and it can miss problems or raise false alarms.
