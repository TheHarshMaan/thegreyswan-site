Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short
  • Manuscripts are checked inside your browser. They are never uploaded to us or stored anywhere.
  • The Grey Swan has no server that receives your data, and no database of users.
  • If you sign in with Google, your saved work is kept in a private app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • You can download or delete everything at any time from My account.

Who we are

The Grey Swan Editorial (“The Grey Swan”, “we”) is an independent project run by Harsh Maan in India, offering free tools for authors and editorial teams at thegreyswan.in. Contact: harsh@thegreyswan.in.

Manuscripts and files you check

Check your manuscript and the reference-list check run entirely in your web browser. The files you add are read on your own device and are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not saved after you close the page.

Reference lookups

To check references (in Check your manuscript, when "Verify references" is ticked, or in the reference-list check), your browser sends each reference's DOI, or its text when there is no DOI, directly from your browser to Crossref, the non-profit DOI registration agency, and downloads a public list of retracted papers. Nothing else from your file is sent. Crossref's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Researcher lookups (Check a person)

Check a person sends the names, ORCID iDs and institution names you enter directly from your browser to OpenAlex, ROR and ORCID, which hold public information about researchers and institutions. Email addresses are compared in your browser and are not sent. Results are not saved anywhere unless you download a report or add someone to your Invitations list.

Reviewer searches (Find reviewers)

Find reviewers reads the manuscript inside your browser. To search, it sends the manuscript's title, keywords, key terms from the abstract and the DOIs in its reference list to OpenAlex, and the authors' and suggested reviewers' names, ORCID iDs and institution names to OpenAlex, ROR and ORCID. To show each candidate's public reviewing record, their ORCID iD (from their OpenAlex profile) is looked up in ORCID's public records. The full manuscript, email addresses and your Invitations list are not sent. The list is read in your browser to show your history with each candidate. Your shortlist, invitation note and settings stay in your browser; results are not saved anywhere unless you download a report or add people to your Invitations list.

Papers you write in Studio

Studio keeps the papers you write in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are not sent to us. If you sign in with Google, they are also kept in the private app folder in your own Google Drive, like your other saved work, so they follow you to other devices. Word files you make or open in Studio are made and read inside your browser. When you look up a reference, the DOI or title you type is sent to Crossref to find it. When you use “Papers you may have missed” or “Find a journal”, your title, keywords and the first words of your abstract are sent to OpenAlex to search; nothing else from your paper is sent. Spreadsheets and images you choose in Studio (for a table, a privacy check or a figure check) are read in your browser and are not saved or uploaded.

Data saved in your browser

The tools save your work (for example, the reviewer tracker and your checker settings) in your browser's local storage, on your device. We cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can use every tool without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • What we ask Google for: your name, email address and profile picture (to show who is signed in), and permission to use a private app folder in your Google Drive (“See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive”). This permission only gives access to files The Grey Swan itself creates in that hidden folder; it cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.
  • What is saved there: your reviewer tracker data, your profile details, your saved journal profiles and preferences, and summaries of past checks (file names, results and dates, but never the manuscripts themselves).
  • Where it goes: straight between your browser and Google. It does not pass through, and is not stored on, any server of ours. Your sign-in is kept in your browser for up to an hour at a time.
  • Other people's details: the reviewer tracker may hold reviewers' names and email addresses that you enter. They are stored only in your browser and your own Google Drive, under your control. Please make sure your use of them is allowed by your journal or employer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Grey Swan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide the saving and syncing features you see in the tools. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not transferred to anyone else, is not read by people, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Your choices

  • Download everything: “Download all my data” in My account.
  • Delete everything: “Delete all my data” in My account removes it from your Google Drive and your browser.
  • Remove access: sign out, or remove The Grey Swan in your Google Account settings.

Cookies, analytics and hosting

We don't set advertising or tracking cookies. To understand how the tools are used, we count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies and doesn't track you across websites. It records the pages visited, the referring site, the browser and screen size, and the country, but not who you are. We also count a few anonymous events, such as "a manuscript check was run" or "a report was downloaded". These counts never include file names, file contents, references, or the names or details of anyone checked.

Google's sign-in window may set its own cookies, under Google's policies. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Feedback

The Feedback button on each page opens a message in your own email (your mail app or Gmail), ready for you to send to us. Nothing is sent until you send it. If you tick "Add the page and browser", the message includes the page address, your browser's name and version, your screen size and the time, to help us find the problem. We use what you send only to reply and to improve the tools.

Cygnus Program applications

The application form on the Cygnus Program page does not send or store anything. It writes an email, with the details you typed, for you to send from your own email account. If you send it, we use your name, institution, department, email address, ORCID iD, subjects and review capacity only to assess and verify your application, including checking your public ORCID and publication record, and to contact you about the program. Ask us at any time to see or delete what you sent.

Children

The tools are meant for researchers, authors and editorial professionals and are not directed at children.
