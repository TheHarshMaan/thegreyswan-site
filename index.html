The Manuscript Pre-Submission Checker

Upload a manuscript (Word, PDF, RTF or LaTeX) and get a clear, colour-coded report in seconds. It recognises research articles, case reports, reviews and letters, and applies the right rules to each.

Structure

IMRaD sections, abstract length and keywords, adjusted for case reports, reviews and letters.

Abbreviations

Used before definition, never defined, defined twice or never used again, in the abstract and the main text.

Figures & tables

Every figure and table cited, in numerical order, with matching legends.

Ethics & consent

Committee name, approval number and date; written, verbal or waived consent; consent for minors.

Study details

Sample size, study period, inclusion and exclusion criteria, questionnaires, and trial or PROSPERO registration where required.

Declarations & references

Conflict of interest, funding, author contributions, data availability, and citations that match the reference list.

Upload

Drop in one or more manuscripts.

Check

Every rule runs in seconds, tuned on a labelled test set of manuscripts.

Fix

See each issue by section, and download the full report as Excel.

Private by design. Files are checked inside your browser and never uploaded; nothing is stored. A screening aid, not a verdict: the checker flags issues for a person to review and does not judge the science.

The Reviewer Tracker

Finding reviewers who say yes is the slowest part of peer review. Log each invitation and its outcome, and the tracker shows who accepts, who delivers, how fast, and who never replies.

Log in seconds

Name, email, keywords and manuscript ID. Update each invitation as replies and reports arrive.

See who delivers

Acceptance rate, reports submitted and average turnaround, with each reviewer marked reliable, mixed or unresponsive.

Find the right people

Enter a manuscript's keywords and get reviewers ranked by expertise and reliability, with recent invites marked.

Your data never leaves your device. Reviewer details are saved in your own browser, not on a server. Download a backup or export to Excel any time.

The Reference Integrity Checker

A retracted paper in the reference list is easy to miss and hard to explain later. Paste a reference list or upload a manuscript, and every reference is checked against Crossref and the Retraction Watch database.

Retractions & corrections

Flags references that have been retracted, corrected or given an expression of concern, with the date and a link to the notice.

DOIs that work

Checks that each DOI exists and points to the paper cited, and finds the DOI for references that don't give one.

Details that match

Compares title, authors and year with the published record, so typos and mix-ups stand out.

Upload or paste

Word, PDF, RTF, LaTeX or plain text, or paste the reference list straight in.

Updated daily

The list of retracted papers is rebuilt every morning from the Retraction Watch database, which now covers more than 60,000 papers.

Download the report

See every reference with its status and how to fix it, then download the results as Excel or Word.

Your manuscript stays on your device. The file is read inside your browser. Only the DOIs and reference titles are sent to Crossref to look them up. A screening aid, not a verdict: check each flagged reference against the notice before acting on it.

About

Built from the screening desk

The Grey Swan is an independent project grounded in hands-on experience of journal screening and peer review: checking manuscripts against journal requirements before review, and finding and inviting the reviewers who assess them.

The same avoidable problems came up again and again: missing ethics details, undefined abbreviations, uncited figures, and reviewer invitations that went unanswered for weeks. These tools turn that experience into checks and trackers anyone can use, so authors can submit with confidence and editorial teams can spend their time on what matters.
