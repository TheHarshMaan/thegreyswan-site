The Cygnus Program

A network of reviewers editors can count on

Verified researchers who commit to a few reviews a year, deliver on time, and get recognised for it. The first cohort is forming now with partner universities in India.

Apply to join Partner as a university

What members get

  • Training. Free peer-review training and a mentored first review.
  • Certificates. A verified record of every completed review, for appraisal and promotion files.
  • Recognition. An opt-in public profile showing your reviews and on-time record.
  • Free manuscript screening. For your own submissions, kept entirely separate from your reviewing.
  • Paid reviews as partners join Reviews arranged with publisher partners are paid.

What we ask

  • Set your capacity. Say how many reviews you can take, say one or two a month, and when you're away.
  • Reply within 48 hours. Yes or no, so editors never wait on silence.
  • Deliver on time. Within the time agreed when you accept.
  • Declare conflicts. Turn down any paper where you know the authors or the work.

Built to be trusted

The rules that keep the network independent, published so editors and members can hold us to them.

  1. We never suggest reviewers for a manuscript we have screened or helped prepare.
  2. Members never review papers from their own institution or from co-authors; overlap is checked before every match.
  3. Every member is verified by institutional email, ORCID and publication record.
  4. Editors invite through their own systems and make every decision.
  5. Your reliability record helps editors choose reviewers. It never affects how your own papers are handled.
