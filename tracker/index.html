Reviewer Tracker

Log every reviewer invitation and its outcome. The tracker works out who accepts, who delivers, how fast, and who never replies, so next time you invite the people most likely to say yes.

Your data stays on this device. Everything is saved in this browser only: nothing is sent to a server, and nobody else can see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and it doesn't move between devices, so download a backup regularly (Backup tab).
